## Workspaces

A workspace groups members, email addresses, and custom domains. Use the workspace switcher to move between workspaces you belong to. Confirm the selected workspace before adding an address or changing settings.

Open **Settings → Workspace Settings** to manage workspace details. Available actions depend on your permissions. Deleting a workspace removes its associated data; review the confirmation carefully before proceeding.

## Members and access

The **Members** settings page lists workspace members. Member-management actions require the corresponding permissions, and address access is assigned separately from workspace membership.

The invitation form currently records an invitation, but automated invitation email delivery is not implemented. Do not assume that submitting the form has sent an email to the recipient.

## Audit logs

Use **Audit Logs** in settings to review recorded workspace activity, such as address creation and domain changes. Access to settings and management actions can differ between members.

## Account sessions

Open **Settings → Authentication** to review your account email, connected sign-in providers, and active sessions. You can revoke an individual session or sign out of all sessions. Revoking your current session requires signing in again.

Your account's sign-in email is separate from the workspace addresses used to receive mail.
