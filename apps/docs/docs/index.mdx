Selfmail brings email addresses, incoming mail, and workspace administration into one dashboard. A workspace keeps its members, addresses, and domains together; your inbox shows mail for the addresses you can access.

## Start here

[Create your first workspace](/getting-started), then send a test email to your new address from another email account.

## Core features

| Feature | What you can do |
| --- | --- |
| [Inbox](/inbox) | Read incoming mail across your addresses or focus on one address. |
| [Email addresses](/addresses) | Create addresses on your workspace domain or a verified custom domain. |
| [Custom domains](/domains) | Connect a domain you own and check its DNS setup. |
| [Workspaces and account](/workspaces) | Switch workspaces, manage workspace settings, and review account sessions. |

## Current availability

These guides describe the current dashboard. AI, workflows, developer tools, contacts, conversations, and analytics have navigation entries but are not yet complete user workflows. The compose panel is also unfinished: opening or closing it does not send an email, and it does not save drafts.
