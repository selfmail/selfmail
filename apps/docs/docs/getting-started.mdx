## Create a workspace

1. Sign in to Selfmail and follow the onboarding flow.
2. Give your workspace a name that you can recognize in the workspace switcher.
3. Choose the handle for your first email address, such as `hello`.
4. Finish creating the workspace and open the dashboard.

Your initial address uses an automatically generated workspace domain, for example `hello@abc1234.selfmail.app`. Copy the actual address shown in the dashboard before sharing it.

The current onboarding flow saves your workspace name and first address. Custom-domain choices and member emails entered during onboarding are not applied yet. Connect your domain afterward through [domain settings](/domains).

## Receive your first email

1. Send a message to your new Selfmail address from another email account.
2. Open the unified inbox, or select the address in the sidebar.
3. Select the message to read it. Opening an unread message marks it as read.

Incoming mail updates the inbox while you are connected. If a message does not appear, confirm the recipient address and refresh the page. For a custom domain, also check its DNS records.
